# NewDermaMed website audit, 9 November 2026

Design, UI/UX, SEO and GEO (AI search) audit of all 192 pages on the Ray-Dev branch, with fixes applied and rebuilt. Nothing is committed yet.

## How it was checked

- **Every page in a real browser, twice:** all 192 pages rendered in headless Chrome at phone width (375 px) and desktop width (1280 px), before and after the fixes. Checked: page renders, console errors, sideways scroll, broken images, missing alt text, headings, duplicate ids, dead links, unlabeled buttons and fields.
- **Flows driven end to end with test data (nothing leaves the computer):** booking form (every step and its validation), cart, checkout and confirmation, site search, mobile menu, shop category filter, add to cart from the pricing page, and links into a section of another page.
- **Static scans of every file:** titles, descriptions, canonicals, structured data, sitemap, robots, llms.txt, internal links, missing files, phone numbers, hours, em dashes, absolute claims, and prescription drug names next to benefit claims.

**Result after the fixes:** no page crashes or renders blank, no sideways scroll at either width, no broken images, no images without alt text, one h1 on every page, and zero JavaScript errors on all 192 pages.

## For the dev team: architecture and performance

1. **Precompile the JSX.** Every page compiles about 450 KB of JSX in the visitor's browser with Babel (about 1.5 MB itself) on every page view. On phones that is the main delay before anything appears. A build step (Vite, esbuild) and one shared, cached JS file would cut most of it.
2. **Build the CSS.** Tailwind's Play CDN generates styles in the browser; Tailwind itself warns it is not for production. Use the Tailwind CLI.
3. **Self-host React, ReactDOM and Lenis.** They load from unpkg.com, so if unpkg is slow or down the site is blank (the static copy from item 1 now shows after 8 seconds). At minimum, add Subresource Integrity hashes.
4. **Videos of 4 to 5 MB** (`hero-2560.mp4`, `botox-.mp4`, `Filler-.mp4`, the two `Fees-Financin.-*.mp4`): re-encode, and serve smaller files to phones.
5. **Pages are 220 to 800 KB of HTML each,** because all shared code is inlined into every page. Moving it into one cached file makes every page after the first nearly free.
6. **Hosting:** HTTPS with HSTS, gzip or brotli, long cache headers for `assets/`, the 301 redirects from `REDIRECTS.md`, and a 404 page (none exists).
7. **Rebuild order** is in `CLAUDE.md`. Two new steps: `home_prices_patch.py` right after `promo_offers_patch.py`, and `tools/prerender.py` last (it needs Google Chrome).
