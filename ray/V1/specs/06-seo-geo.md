# 06. SEO and GEO (search and AI answers)

Everything below is generated by `NDM/src/patches-applied/seo_patch.py`, which runs last after every rebuild. Keep the same output in production, whatever generates it.

## Per page, in the head

Between `<!-- seo -->` and `<!-- /seo -->`:

- `<html lang="en-CA">`
- canonical address: `https://newdermamedlaserclinic.com/<file>` (the home page is the bare domain)
- robots: `index,follow,max-image-preview:large`; kept out of search: `cart.html`, `checkout.html`, `order-confirmed.html` and `black-friday.html` (campaign on hold), which set their own `noindex, nofollow`, and `index-fraunces.html` (obsolete, `noindex,follow`)
- `geo.region` CA-ON and `geo.placename` Toronto
- Open Graph (site name, locale en_CA, type, address, title, description, image) and Twitter card (large image); the image is the page's own photo: the treatment or concern hero, the article or product image, or the logo card (`assets/icons/logo-1200x630.png`)
- a JSON-LD graph with `id="ld-site"` (below)

The title and description are each page's own: the live site's wording on the treatment, shop and article pages (dashes turned into commas as approved), new wording on the concern pages. The pages' earlier structured data stays as well: `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage` on treatment and concern pages, `BlogPosting` on articles, `Product` with `Offer` on shop products, clinic data on `about.html`.

## What matters most in production

1. **Serve the page text in the HTML.** In the prototype the visible text is rendered in the browser. AI assistants' crawlers and link previews mostly do not run JavaScript, so without pre-rendering they only see the head. Pre-render or server-render every page.
2. **Keep the facts identical everywhere:** clinic names, addresses, phone numbers and hours must match across the pages, the structured data, `llms.txt` and the Google Business Profiles.
3. **Keep the quotable lines.** Treatment pages open their tab card with an "In short" sentence built only from the page's facts and price list; concern pages have one in the "On our site" card. Keep the FAQ sections as real text with their `FAQPage` data.
4. **Final addresses.** The prototype uses file names at the root (`juvederm.html`). If production uses clean paths (for example `/juvederm/`), change the address pattern in one place (`url = SITE + file` in the patch, or the CMS permalinks), update `REDIRECTS.md`, and keep every old live address answering with a 301 to its new page.
5. **Images:** the live photos had empty alt texts; they now say what they show ("Juvéderm® at NewDermaMed, Toronto"). Keep alt texts on every content image; decorative images keep empty alts.
